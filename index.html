Novena Supplication for the Holy Souls

Divine Jesus, have mercy on the souls in purgatory. O Jesus, Son of King David! Looking at the cruel sufferings of Your holy wounds increased by the weight of the cross, have mercy on the souls in purgatory. Lord, have mercy on these souls, just as You showed grace to the repentant thief when You offered Yourself as a sacrifice on the altar of the cross. Opening Prayer Eternal Father, I offer Thee the Precious Blood, passion, and death of Thy Divine Son, Jesus Christ, in reparation for the sins of the Holy Souls in Purgatory. Supplication for the Souls O most sweet Jesus, through the agony You suffered in the Garden of Gethsemane, have mercy on the souls in Purgatory. O most sweet Jesus, through the cruel scourging You endured at the pillar, have mercy on the souls in Purgatory. O most sweet Jesus, through the painful crowning with thorns You suffered, have mercy on the souls in Purgatory. O most sweet Jesus, through the sorrow You suffered in carrying Your heavy cross, have mercy on the souls in Purgatory. O most sweet Jesus, through the bitter agony of Your holy death upon the cross, have mercy on the souls in Purgatory.
